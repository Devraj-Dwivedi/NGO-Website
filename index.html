About Our Sansthan

Walking Beside the Marginalized as their Saarthi

Saarthi Aadarsh Seva Sansthan is committed to building a society where every individual — regardless of caste, creed, or economic status — has access to education, healthcare, and livelihood opportunities. We walk alongside the marginalized as their 'Saarthi' — their charioteer — guiding them toward a dignified future.

Mission

Empowering rural communities of Uttar Pradesh through education, healthcare, and sustainable livelihood programs.

Vision

A self-reliant, equitable India where no child goes to bed hungry, no family lacks shelter, and every woman stands empowered — by 2035.

What We Do

Our Core Objectives

Six pillars guiding our humanitarian mission across rural Uttar Pradesh.

Education for All

Providing free schooling, books, uniforms, and tutoring to underprivileged children in rural UP.

Health & Medical Aid

Organizing free medical camps, distributing medicines, and connecting patients with specialist care.

Women Empowerment

Vocational training, self-help groups, and legal awareness programs for rural women.

Livelihood Support

Skill development, micro-enterprise support, and agriculture training for unemployed youth.

Child Welfare

Child nutrition programs, vaccination drives, and protection of child rights in underserved communities.

Community Development

Sanitation drives, rural infrastructure support, and disaster relief in times of crisis.

Our Initiatives

Flagship projects transforming lives across 18+ villages in Uttar Pradesh.

EducationOngoing

Bal Vidya Abhiyan

A district-wide initiative launched in 2019 providing free educational kits, remedial classes, and digital literacy to 1,200+ children across 18 villages in Bahosi block.

HealthcareOngoing

Aarogya Setu Medical Camp

Bi-monthly free health camps offering consultations, blood tests, medicines, and specialist referrals. Over 8,500 beneficiaries since 2017.

EmpowermentOngoing

Shakti — Women's Skill Hub

A women-centered skill training program in tailoring, handicrafts, and computer literacy, helping 400+ women achieve financial independence since 2020.

Meet Our Team

The dedicated individuals driving Saarthi's mission forward.

GS

Ganesh Sanap

Founder & President

Shri Ganesh Sanap, a visionary social reformer from Maharashtra, founded Saarthi Aadarsh Seva Sansthan in 2015 driven by a lifelong commitment to uplifting the rural poor. he has dedicated over two decades to grassroots humanitarian service. His leadership has shaped the organization's philosophy that sustainable change begins at the village level.

Co-Founder & Vice President

Shri Sanjeev Kumar brings 18 years of experience in rural administration and community mobilization. A dedicated public servant by training, he co-founded the organization to bridge the gap between government welfare schemes and last-mile beneficiaries.
